Step 1: Clean your ResClient/Data folder.
Step 2: In Resource folder find Rijndael_res and open it. Remove every line and save.
Step 3: In Resource Folder find Rijndael_Add and open it. Remove every line and copy 1 
the list in here to Rijndael_add and save.
Step 4: Open -Aes Crypt. Click Scan file. Once its done scaning click Encrypt.
Step 5: Open ResClient/Data And copy all new WITHOUT .AUTH files!! to the respective folder in Client.
